Why it exists D&C 4:3

The account posts short videos about the things that come up most: who Jesus Christ is, whether God still calls prophets, why Latter-day Saints build temples, what family history is actually for, and what the Second Coming does and does not mean.

It is not an official Church account and does not speak for the Church.
