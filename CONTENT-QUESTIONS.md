# For Tanner — what the site still needs

The site is built and every page is laid out. What is missing is the part only you can supply.
Nothing has been invented on your behalf: anywhere a fact was unknown, the page shows a small
dashed box reading "What would you like here?" instead of a guess. This document says what each
of those boxes is actually asking for.

Answer as much or as little as you like — anything left blank just stays a dashed box until it
is filled. Written answers can be rough; they will be set into the page as-is unless you ask
otherwise.

Run `grep -rn 'class="todo"' *.html` at any time to see exactly what is still open.

## 3. My Story — `story.html`

Six chapters. Each one has a title and a scripture in place and nothing else — the framing
paragraphs that used to sit here were removed, because they were guesses about your life
written by someone who has not lived it. Write in the first person; it will read as your
voice, because it is.

- [ ] **01 Before** — where you grew up and who was in the house. Was the Church already part of
      family life, or did it arrive later? One specific childhood memory you now recognise as the
      start of something. *4–6 sentences.*
- [ ] **02 A Testimony of My Own** — when it stopped being your parents' faith and became yours.
      What made you ask honestly for the first time. What happened when you did. *6–10 sentences.
      This is the chapter people will read most carefully.*
- [ ] **03 The Call** — deciding to go, and what you gave up to go. Opening the call letter: where
      you were, who was there, what you expected. Then the MTC. *6–8 sentences.*
- [ ] **04 Tennessee** — the short version of two years. Which area changed you. The hardest month
      and what got you through it. One person you will not forget — **first name only**, unless
      they have said yes to more. What you were wrong about on day one. *8–12 sentences.*
- [ ] **05 Coming Home** — what the first month home was actually like. Not the airport; the week
      after. Returned missionaries reading this will know if it has been polished. *5–8 sentences.*
- [ ] **06 Now** — what you are doing, why you started The Great Mediator, and what you want to be
      doing in ten years. *5–8 sentences.*

## 4. Testimony — `testimony.html`

One box, and it is the most important text on the site. Deliberately left completely empty.

- [ ] Your testimony, **in your own words, first person, unedited by anyone else.** Worth
      covering: Jesus Christ and what He has actually done for you; the Book of Mormon and how
      you came to trust it; Joseph Smith; whether God answers you when you pray, and how you
      would describe that to someone who has never felt it; what you would say to a person who
      wants this to be true but is not sure. *3–6 paragraphs.*

Say what you *know* rather than what the Church teaches — the second is already covered on the
Beliefs page. Do not polish it into something that sounds like everyone else's. The only reason
to put a testimony on a website is that it is yours.
