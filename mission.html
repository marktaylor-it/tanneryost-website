The Mission

Two years in the American South, knocking on doors that mostly did not open.

Latter-day Saint missionaries pay their own way, go where they are assigned rather than where they ask, and spend two years working for nothing. They are given a companion, an area, a bicycle or a car, and a name tag that puts the name of Jesus Christ above their own.

The guitar D&C 25:12

He learned about the love of Christ by getting good at something, and then giving it away.

Partway through the mission, the mission’s own account — Believe., based in Nashville — filmed Elder Yost with a guitar, talking about what practising an instrument had taught him about how Christ loves people. It is a short clip and an unusually unguarded one.

It is also the only piece of his mission that exists publicly, which is why it is here rather than a summary written after the fact.
